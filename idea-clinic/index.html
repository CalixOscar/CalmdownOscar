WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

  1. It looks for similar apps, including any you named.
  2. It asks about your weak spots, one question at a time. “I don’t know” is still a fine reply.
  3. It looks back from a year ahead, as if it already happened: why it worked, why it might have stalled, and what would have prevented it.
  4. It writes a decision brief: what you know and what you’re guessing, a recommendation, and one small test to run next.
  1. It checks whether you need to build anything. An existing app, a Shortcut or a notes template might already do the job.
  2. If it’s worth building, it asks up to three short questions.
  3. It gives you a tiny plan: the one job, what to leave out, and what to do in your first sitting.
  1. It checks the opening is real, by looking for anything close. If something exists, better to know on day one.
  2. It checks it can be built with today’s technology and within Apple and Android’s limits. If nobody has built it, there may be a reason.
  3. It asks what would make this spread, up to three short questions.
  4. It looks back from two years ahead: the story of how it spread, and what could have stalled it.
  5. It writes your plan: the first version, how it spreads, what it would take, and three steps for this week.

If it agrees with everything, ask it: “What’s the strongest reason not to build this?”
