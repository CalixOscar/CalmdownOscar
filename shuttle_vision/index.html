03 / User Guide Step-by-Step Instructions

How to Get Started on Court

Set up in under 60 seconds with zero network configuration.

Mount the Phone — Don't Hold It

Shuttle Vision tracks the shuttle by measuring how it moves between frames, so it assumes the camera itself is holding still. A handheld phone adds its own motion to every measurement: the shuttle's apparent speed and direction pick up your hand's shake, rolling shutter skews fast movement, and the tracker loses the shuttle more often mid-flight. On a tripod, everything that moves in frame is genuinely moving. Any steady mount works — a tripod, a clamp on the net post, or the phone propped against a bag — but handheld is the one setup we don't recommend for rally counting or line review.

Guide 01

Solo & Casual Match

1. Mount your iPhone horizontally on a tripod at the umpire chair or side of court (~1.2–1.6m high) so it sees the full court.
2. Tap Casual → Quick Start Match, or open Tournament → Solo Umpire Match to name the players first.
3. Tap or swipe up (+1) to score. Tap Umpire Review at any moment to settle a contested line call with slow-mo frame stepping.

Guide 02

Multi-Phone Court Rig

1. Host taps Tournament → Multi-Phone Mesh, picks Duo or Pro, then taps Pair with Other Phones to show the court QR code.
2. The second phone scans that code (or types the join code shown beneath it).
3. Place the phones at opposing net poles (~40cm high). Both cameras synchronize clocks automatically over direct P2P.

Guide 03

Master Tournament Desk

1. Open Shuttle Vision on an iPad and tap Tournament → Tournament Desk & Standings → Create Tournament.
2. Paste your player roster and generate Round Robin, Single Elimination or Open Court Queue fixtures.
3. Open the Master Desk and tap Connect Court Phone. Umpires scan the code, pick their court (Court 1–8), and begin live scorekeeping.
