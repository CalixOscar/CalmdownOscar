Working with AI

Mac

Unli Rice

Shared memory for Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and other AI tools, and a To Do list of what’s still open.

Price
Free, open source
Connects
Over the Model Context Protocol (MCP)
Data
Notes live on your Mac
Mac App StoreProduct pageSource
  1. 1Not backed up. Work in a granted project folder that exists only on this Mac.
  2. 2Next step. What the project’s own notes say should happen next.
  3. 3Suggested by AI. Follow-ups an assistant filed, with who suggested them and when.
  4. 4Not merged yet. Finished work that isn’t part of the main version.
  1. 01
    Identity, intent and guardrails

    A profile and house rules every connected assistant reads at the start of a session.

  2. 02
    Status from the work itself

    Read from the folders you grant and the branches each repository already knows. It doesn’t fetch or count commits.

  3. 03
    Propose, never apply

    Notes are append-only and conflicts are flagged for a person. It never edits another tool’s configuration; you paste a block yourself.

Photos & storage

One photo engine, two apps.

iPhone · iPad

ClearSpace

Biggest photos and videos first. Heart the keepers, clear the rest.

Price
Free to review; bulk clearing $9.99, once. Family Sharing.
Data
On-device, no account
App StoreProduct pagePrivacy
  1. 01
    Codec-aware size, not pixel count

    Size is estimated from each file’s codec, dimensions and duration, so a 40,000-item library sorts without opening a file.

  2. 02
    Non-destructive by design

    Every deletion goes to Recently Deleted, with 30 days to undo.

  3. 03
    Grid and photo side by side

    On iPad, a tapped photo opens beside the grid.

Mac

Unli Disk

Map what fills your Mac, including developer caches and photo libraries.

Price
$14.99, once. Includes ClearSpace Pro, sent as a one-time App Store code on request.
Data
On-device
Mac App StoreProduct pagePrivacy
  1. 01
    Not just folder sizes

    Finds orphaned Git repositories and developer caches, not just large folders.

  2. 02
    Trash only, never unlink

    Every deletion goes through the macOS Trash.

  3. 03
    Signal, not a model

    Duplicates and blur are found with a perceptual hash, Apple Vision feature prints and a blur score. An on-device vision model was tried and cut.

Wedding planning

iPhone · iPad · Mac

Wedding Concierge

Guests, seating, budget, vendors and RSVPs on one board, with an AI concierge.

Data
Syncs with your iCloud. No tracking.
Answers
From real map and place listings
Live
Since August 7, 2026
App StoreMac App Storenuptia.wedding
  1. 01
    Grounded, not generative

    Venue and vendor picks come from Apple MapKit and Google Places, not the model’s memory.

  2. 02
    No key in the app

    Cloud calls go through a relay that holds the API key. On-device models were tried and pulled; it runs Gemini Flash.

  3. 03
    Local-first, minimal data

    SwiftData and CloudKit sync plans across devices. No backend database.

Data
Offline. Phones link directly, no server.
App StoreProduct pagePrivacy
  1. 01
    Nobody agrees on the score

    A few rallies into doubles, nobody is sure who serves or from which box.

  2. 02
    Serve, box, ends and score

Curiosity

Web

OpenGrail

695 traditions and 1,088 links between them.

Price
Free, MIT licensed
Data
No accounts or analytics
Open the atlasSource
  1. 01
    Markdown in, graph out

    Every tradition is a Markdown file; a build script generates the graph.

  2. 02
    A claim is derived from a source, or removed

    A generated first pass pinned 127 traditions near Jerusalem, Quakers included. Origins now come from Wikidata, and 248 unsourced images were deleted.

  3. 03
    Static

    No backend, analytics, accounts or AI service.
