# INTENT-002 — Norsksti guided classroom flow

**Date:** 2026-10-25
**Author:** Founder request, recorded by Codex from the existing plan/revision brief; no new discovery interview.
**Status:** recorded for the authorised pilot; educational drafts and pilot arrangements remain open.
**Plan:** docs/PLAN-norsksti-guided-flow.md

## The problem

Norsksti presents a worksheet and many exercises together. The founder wants to retain its features while replacing this dense experience with a guided flow: finish one meaningful task, see feedback, then continue. It must support a teacher leading a class and a beginner studying alone without adding hundreds of unnecessary Next clicks.

## Who hits it, and when

A teacher needs to direct everyone to the same task and revisit a worksheet example. A learner needs to understand the next action, keep their attempt when moving away and resume after interruption. Someone studying alone still follows the teacher-managed course's release and approval rules.

## What “solved” looks like

- Related short items stay together, with individual checking and one Continue; exact-key mismatches do not trap learners.
- Worksheet context, saved attempts and unfinished tasks are easy to find. Showing a key preserves the attempt until explicitly using the answer.
- All courses have consistent derived flow. Course 1 receives short draft goals/cues before teacher-led and solo pilot feedback informs further authoring.
- A teacher directs by visible step labels and Outline jumps without recording demonstrations as practice.
- Practice completion, downloading a review report and teacher approval remain visibly different. Existing tools and original lesson/homework assignment remain intact.

Observable check: a teacher leads a session and a solo beginner completes/resumes work, locates context and explains the next action/approval boundary without coaching. Not yet performed `(unverified)`.

## How it should feel

A calm teaching stage with one clear purpose/action, readable context and honest feedback. Related drills stay together. Avoid a generic slide deck, decorative checkpoints, forced correctness gates or a screen for every blank.

## Scope boundaries

Pilot code: derived flow for every course, Course 1 overrides, local draft safety and classroom presentation. No new backend, dependency, AI marking, generated audio, live sync, report/progress schema change, deployment or saleable template. Course 2–10 override authoring waits for actual pilot evidence. Existing teacher approval remains when studying alone.
