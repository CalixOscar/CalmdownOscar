A motorcycle assistant that runs in your browser

Requirement: WebGPU support and unified memory (M1+ iPad/Mac or desktop GPU recommended). The model downloads once and runs in your browser, so inference happens on your device. Your messages do not stay there: each one is also used as a search query, sent through this site's server to Serper to fetch the pictures and clips beside it, and the media loads from whoever hosts it.

Boot the AI Engine to begin. Ask about any motorcycle model (e.g. Ducati Superleggera V4, BMW R1300GS, Yamaha R1).

The hosted version: Chatty by Veritio Digital

The chat window on this page is Chatty: live chat with an AI chatbot trained on your own docs, FAQs and site content, so people get an answer at 2am instead of a contact form. Installing it is one script tag — that's the whole integration, which is exactly how this kind of thing should feel. You'll also find it as a small chat bubble in the bottom-right corner of this page.

Veritio Digital runs it as a hosted product: bot and human in the same inbox, a hand-off to a real person when the bot reaches its limit, and a 30-day free trial to see whether it earns its place. Worth a look if you'd rather answer a question once than a hundred times.
