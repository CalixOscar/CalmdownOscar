Chatty, by Veritio Digital

The window above is Chatty: live chat with an AI chatbot trained on your own docs, FAQs and site content, so people get an answer at 2am instead of a contact form. Installing it is one script tag — that's the whole integration, which is exactly how this kind of thing should feel. You'll also find it as a small chat bubble in the bottom-right corner of this page.

Veritio Digital runs it as a hosted product: bot and human in the same inbox, a hand-off to a real person when the bot reaches its limit, and a 30-day free trial to see whether it earns its place. Worth a look if you'd rather answer a question once than a hundred times.
