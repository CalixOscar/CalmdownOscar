Tinda helps home sellers take orders through one list link. We collect as little as we can and delete it on a schedule.

What we collect

  • When you order: your name, your unit or address, your phone number (optional), and the payment reference number you enter.
  • When you sell: your email address (for sign-in), your shop name, how buyers can reach you, and your payment QR image. When you pay for Tinda we keep the reference number you sent, the amount, the dates you paid and were confirmed, and a record of any refund or change the owner made. These are payment records. They are not deleted automatically while your shop exists. If you add a photo to your preview picture, it is shown to anyone who has your list link. The picture in a chat preview can be up to two hours behind on what is sold out. The list page itself is always current.

Why

So the seller can prepare your order, deliver it and confirm your payment. We don’t use your details for ads, we don’t sell them, and Tinda has no trackers or analytics.

Who sees it

  • The seller you ordered from.
  • Tinda, as the service, and our hosting providers, Supabase (database and files) and Vercel (website), which process it for us.

Where it’s kept

On servers in Singapore.

How long

  • Orders are deleted automatically 60 days after the order day. The cleanup runs daily, so it happens within 61 days.
  • Your phone keeps a link to your receipt for 7 days, then removes it.
  • While an order is unpaid, we keep a scrambled code made from the network address it came from, so one connection can’t hold all of a seller’s stock. We store a scrambled code made from the address, not the address itself. The scrambling changes every day. The code is erased when the order is paid, cancelled or released.
  • We count requests per network address, also as a scrambled code, to stop abuse. Those counts are deleted within 2 days.

Deleting sooner

  • Buyers: after the order day, open your receipt and tap “Delete my order”. You can also ask the seller, or email us at help@calmdownoscar.com from the receipt link. We confirm the request with the receipt link or with the seller.
  • Sellers: email us to close your shop. We delete your shop, menus, orders and QR image.

Backups

Our database provider keeps backups for a limited time, so a deleted order can stay in a backup until that copy expires. If a backup is ever restored, we run the deletion again straight away.

Payments

Tinda never handles buyers’ money. Buyers pay the seller directly with the seller’s own GCash or Maya QR. The only money Tinda receives is the seller’s own fee for using Tinda.

The law

We follow the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). To ask about your data, email help@calmdownoscar.com.
