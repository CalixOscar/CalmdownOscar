Tinda helps home sellers take orders through one list link. We collect as little as we can and delete it on a schedule.

What we collect

  • When you order: your name, your unit or address, your phone number (optional), a short note to the seller (optional), and the payment reference number you enter. The note is kept only with the order and is deleted with it.
  • When you sell: your email address (for sign-in), your shop name, how buyers can reach you, and your payment QR images. Your backup GCash number, if you add one. Buyers see it only on their order page while payment is due. It is deleted with your shop. When you pay for Tinda we keep the reference number you sent, the amount, the dates you paid and were confirmed, and a record of any refund or change the owner made. These are payment records. They are not deleted automatically while your shop exists. If you add a photo to your preview picture, it is shown to anyone who has your list link. The picture in a chat preview can be up to two hours behind on what is sold out. The list page itself is always current. If we pause a shop after a report, we keep a scrambled copy of its sign-in email so it can’t open a new shop while paused, and for up to 1 year after the shop is deleted. We keep a record of pause decisions for 1 year. When you open a shop we record that you agreed to our terms and when. We keep that record until your account is deleted.

Why

So the seller can prepare your order, deliver it and confirm your payment. We don’t use your details for ads, we don’t sell them, and Tinda has no trackers or analytics.

Who sees it

  • The seller you ordered from.
  • If a seller sets a pickup place, only buyers who ordered pickup see it, on their receipt.
  • Tinda, as the service, and our hosting providers, Supabase (database and files) and Vercel (website), which process it for us.

Where it’s kept

On servers in Singapore.

How long

  • Orders are deleted automatically 60 days after the order day. The cleanup runs daily, so it happens within 61 days.
  • Your phone keeps a link to your receipt for 7 days, then removes it.
  • While an order is unpaid, we keep a scrambled code made from the network address it came from, so one connection can’t hold all of a seller’s stock. We store a scrambled code made from the address, not the address itself. The scrambling changes every day. The code is erased when the order is paid, cancelled or released.
  • We count requests per network address, also as a scrambled code, to stop abuse. Those counts are deleted within 2 days.

Reports

If you report a problem, we keep your email, the reason, what you wrote, and a copy of the order’s items, total, status and reference number. We use your email only to reply about that report. We delete a report 90 days after we close it, or 7 days after we dismiss it. If we haven’t finished with a report, we delete it 180 days after it was sent. Copies of our replies stay in our mailbox, and our email provider keeps delivery records. A report stays even if the order or shop is deleted, until those times. Our moderation log (what we decided about a shop, with a short note) is kept for 1 year. If a report is about illegal or harmful content, we may put the shop on hold. We then keep a copy of the shop’s details, lists, pictures, orders (including buyers’ names, addresses, phone numbers, notes and payment references) and reports, even if an order or the shop would otherwise be deleted, until we release the hold. We may give that copy to the authorities. After release we delete the copy and keep only a note that there was a hold, for 1 year.

Deleting sooner

  • Buyers: after the order day, open your receipt and tap “Delete my order”. You can also ask the seller, or email us at help@calmdownoscar.com from the receipt link. We confirm the request with the receipt link or with the seller.
  • Sellers: tap Delete my shop on your shop home. We delete your shop, lists and orders straight away, then your payment QR, photos and sign-in; if that last step fails, we finish it as soon as we can. Link previews already shared in chats may show your old list for up to about 2 hours, and we can’t remove copies in other people’s chats or phones. We keep a scrambled copy of your link name for 90 days so nobody else can take it, and short-lived security counters expire within 2 days.

Backups

Our database provider keeps backups for a limited time, so a deleted order can stay in a backup until that copy expires. If a backup is ever restored, we run the deletion again straight away.

Payments

Tinda never handles buyers’ money. Buyers pay the seller directly with the seller’s own GCash or Maya QR. The only money Tinda receives is the seller’s own fee for using Tinda.

The law

We follow the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). To ask about your data, email help@calmdownoscar.com.
