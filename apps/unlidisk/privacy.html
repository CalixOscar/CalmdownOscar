Unli Disk Privacy Policy

Last updated August 3, 2026

100% On-Device Privacy

Unli Disk is designed with a strict privacy-first architecture. All file scanning, disk measurements, and photo library similarity analyses occur locally on your Mac. No files, metadata, telemetry, or personal information leave your device.

No Trackers or Analytics

Unli Disk contains zero third-party analytics SDKs, trackers, or advertising frameworks. We do not collect or store any telemetry data.

File System & Photo Access

Unli Disk requests macOS Sandbox Powerbox permissions and Photo Library access solely to calculate storage footprint and allow you to manage files. Deletions move files safely to Finder Trash or Apple Photos Recently Deleted.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact peter@calmdownoscar.com.
