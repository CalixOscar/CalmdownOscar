Unli Disk User Guide

How to navigate the Visual Disk Map, clear Developer build artifacts, and sweep your Photo Library.

1. Visual Disk Map

Click Choose Disk… to grant Powerbox access to your Macintosh HD volume or specific folders. Unli Disk presents an interactive disk-proportioned treemap showing exactly where your storage is allocated.

2. Developer & System Reclaim

Select category chips (Xcode Simulators, DerivedData, Swift Build Caches, Package Caches, Git Leftovers). Click Reclaim Space to clean up unnecessary build artifacts safely.

3. Photo Library Sweep

Switch to the Photo Library tab. Unli Disk indexes photos and videos biggest-first. Double-tap a photo tile or click the heart icon to keep it. Filter into Finishable Month Cohorts or run Duplicates & blur detection to streamline review.

4. Safe Recovery

Deletions move items safely to macOS Finder Trash or Apple Photos Recently Deleted, preserving a 30-day recovery window.
