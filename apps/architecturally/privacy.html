Architecturally Privacy Policy

Last updated August 10, 2026

Summary

  • Floor plans and concepts are stored on your device, not on a server we control.
  • Your AI credit balance is stored in your own private iCloud account via CloudKit.
  • AI features send only the relevant text or video URL to our backend, which forwards it to Google's Gemini API to generate a response. We don't retain this content after the request completes.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • We never sell or share your data for advertising.

Floor plans and projects

Every concept, floor plan, and manual plan you create is stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage. We do not have a server-side database of your projects, and we cannot see them.

AI credits

Architecturally uses Apple CloudKit to store your AI credit balance in the private portion of your own iCloud account. This data is scoped to your Apple ID and isn't accessible to us. You need to be signed in to iCloud for credits to sync across your devices.

AI-generated content

When you generate a concept, edit a plan with a typed or spoken command, or reconstruct a plan from a YouTube video, the app sends the relevant input — your brief text, canvas command, or the video URL — to our backend API, hosted on Vercel. The backend forwards this to Google's Gemini API to produce a response, then returns it to your device. We don't retain a copy once a request is answered. Video URLs go directly to Gemini for inspection; Architecturally never downloads or stores the video itself. An anonymous, per-device identifier tied to your iCloud account may be included solely to verify and debit your AI credit balance.

Microphone and speech recognition

Voice commands on the canvas request microphone and speech-recognition access. Audio is used only to transcribe your spoken command into text via Apple's Speech framework — it's not stored or used for any other purpose.

In-app purchases

AI credit purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment information.

Data we do not collect

No advertising identifiers, analytics events, crash reports with personal data, contacts, location, photos, or browsing history. Architecturally contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Data retention

Local project data persists on your device until you delete it in the app or delete the app itself. CloudKit credit data persists in your private iCloud database until you delete it via iCloud settings or contact us. AI request content is not retained by our backend after a response is returned.

Children's privacy

Architecturally is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: peter@calmdownoscar.com.
