Architecturally User Guide

How to start a concept, draw and edit a plan, reconstruct one from video, preview it in 3D, and export a Concept Pack.

1. Projects home

Saved concepts live on the Projects screen, alongside three starting points: Start a conversation, Start from scratch, and Video to plan. Two sample homes are open to explore and remix immediately.

2. Start a conversation

Describe your dream home by typing or speaking — bedrooms, lot size, priorities, dealbreakers. Architecturally turns it into a structured concept brief with visible assumptions, then a first concept. Hard requirements stay attached as you keep exploring styles, 3D, or exports.

3. Draw and edit on the canvas

Use the Wall, Opening, and Room tools to sketch with Apple Pencil or a finger — lines snap into place. Switch to Select to tap a room and edit it, or drag it into a new arrangement. Pinch to zoom, drag to pan, and undo any step.

4. Voice commands

Tap the microphone on the canvas and speak an edit — "make the primary bedroom bigger," "move the garage to the left." The assistant proposes the change and explains its reasoning before applying it.

5. Video to plan

From Projects, tap Video to plan and paste a public YouTube URL (standard videos or Shorts). Gemini inspects visible walls, doors, and room order, then builds a rough, editable plan — flagging anything it couldn't see clearly. This uses AI credits; the video itself is never downloaded or stored.

6. Rough 3D preview

Tap 3D on any plan to see it extruded into a roofless massing model, live from the drawing. Orbit, pan, and zoom to judge scale and flow.

7. Manage concepts

Long-press a concept card to delete it. If unfinished drafts pile up, a Clear drafts button appears on Projects to remove them all at once — completed manual plans are never touched.

8. Export a Concept Pack

From an open concept, export a portable folder with a concept summary PDF, floor programme, a labelled 3D still, a style direction, and a vendor-neutral visual prompt — ready for Files or a professional. Nothing uploads automatically.

9. AI credits

Every iCloud account starts with 20 free credits, synced privately through your own iCloud. Drawing, editing, saving, and rough 3D previews are always free. Check your balance and buy more from the credits icon in the top bar.
