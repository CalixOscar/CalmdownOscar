calm down, oscar.

I'm Peter. Oscar is my son — he's the reason this studio has a name, and he's still working on the calm part.

My head runs on a hundred ideas a week, very few of them make it as far as the App Store. The ones that do are often tools based on my own needs as I develop, or how to handle a work-life balance with my son and a full-time job in busy Metro Manila.

I love a good challenge and the thrill of piecing systems together. My experience ranges from SAP and Microsoft Foundry to trying to make games in Roblox. I am sort of techy yet no specialist, but more of an overseer of things as I see how systems can connect and integrate.

When I'm not shipping software, I dream of riding motorcycles.
